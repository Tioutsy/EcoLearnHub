# Pilot-to-Commercial Boundary (Sprint 10E)

## Executive Summary
This document establishes strict operational boundaries between pilot participants and commercial customers.

---

## Operational Boundaries

1. **Automatic Conversion Prohibition**: A completed pilot never converts into a paying customer automatically.
2. **Commercial Customer Prerequisites**: Stage 8 (`stage_8_commercial_customer_confirmed`) status requires:
   - Formally executed subscription contract.
   - Selected monthly employee band (Band 1 to Band 5).
   - Billing details and primary commercial signatory recorded.
   - Database record update `recordEnvironment = "commercial"`.
