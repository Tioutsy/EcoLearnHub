# ELH-23 — Workplace Sustainability Initiatives Duplication and Progression Matrix

## Executive Summary

- **Course**: ELH-23 — Workplace Sustainability Initiatives
- **Target Audience**: Employees, Green Team members, Department Leads, and Operations Managers
- **Objective**: Establish the specific capability to transform a sustainability idea or opportunity into a controlled, feasible, approved, piloted, measured, and reviewed workplace initiative.
- **Core Progression Distinction**: While ELH-13 provides multi-action planning mechanics across departmental goals and ELH-17 tracks task statuses, **ELH-23 specifically governs single-initiative end-to-end change control** (problem definition, scope boundaries, feasibility testing, sponsor approval, pilot execution, evidence evaluation, review decision, and operational closeout).

---

## Detailed Duplication and Progression Matrix

| Course Code & Title | Existing Capability | ELH-23 Unique Workplace Capability Added | Duplication Prevention Safeguards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ELH-13** Sustainability Action Planning | Multi-action planning across departmental goals | Governs a single defined initiative from problem identification, evidence, feasibility, sponsor approval, pilot testing, review decision, to closeout. | ELH-13 focuses on task registers and timelines; ELH-23 focuses on initiative change control, scope boundaries, and pilot decisions. |
| **ELH-14** Setting Departmental Sustainability Goals | Setting departmental KPIs and annual targets | Converts a high-level departmental goal into a scoped, controlled delivery initiative with explicit exclusions. | ELH-14 defines *what* target to hit; ELH-23 defines *how* to control and pilot a specific delivery initiative to hit it. |
| **ELH-15** Building Workplace Sustainability Team | Establishing and operating a sustainability team | Teaches how a sustainability team evaluates, briefs, sponsors, and pilots a specific workplace initiative. | ELH-15 teaches team structure and charter; ELH-23 teaches the initiative delivery process executed by the team. |
| **ELH-17** Tracking Sustainability Actions & Progress | Tracking owners, target dates, status, and evidence | Coordinates cross-functional milestones, operational dependencies, and pilot trade-offs across initiative stages. | ELH-17 tracks action line items; ELH-23 manages the overall initiative lifecycle gate decisions (Proceed, Pilot, Modify, Stop). |
| **ELH-18** Sustainability Data Collection & Evidence | Gathering raw energy, water, waste, and carbon data | Selects specific baseline data and result indicators required to evaluate pilot outcome evidence. | ELH-18 teaches data gathering methodologies; ELH-23 teaches selecting data to evaluate initiative success vs activity metrics. |
| **ELH-19** Reviewing Sustainability Performance | Reviewing departmental performance against baselines | Conducts a formal review decision (Embed into SOPs, Scale, Modify, Pause, or Close) for a completed pilot. | ELH-19 reviews broad performance results; ELH-23 reviews pilot evidence to make operational handover or scale-up decisions. |
| **ELH-20** Roles & Governance | Assigning organizational responsibility & escalation | Defines specific initiative roles: Initiative Owner, Sponsor, Approver, Supporting Roles, and Data Owner. | ELH-20 establishes overall governance; ELH-23 applies role governance to initiative approval gates and budgets. |
| **ELH-21** Employee Engagement | Workplace engagement & psychological safety | Uses employee consultation during scope definition and pilot feedback to ensure operational usability. | ELH-21 teaches engagement principles; ELH-23 applies employee consultation to test feasibility and avoid unintended workarounds. |
| **ELH-22** Effective Green Teams | Green team meeting facilitation & action delivery | Applies green team collaboration to initiative problem briefing and pilot observation. | ELH-22 manages team meeting hygiene; ELH-23 delivers structured workplace initiatives. |

## Key Framework Introduced in ELH-23

The **INITIATE** Operational Framework:
1. **I**dentify real need & baseline condition
2. **N**ame intended outcome & scope boundaries
3. **I**nvolve owners, sponsors & affected stakeholders
4. **T**est feasibility, risks & operational dependencies
5. **I**mplement through controlled pilot
6. **A**ssess evidence & unintended effects
7. **T**ake a review decision (Embed, Scale, Modify, Stop)
8. **E**mbed learning & close responsibly into SOPs
