## Release Blockers Identified

1. **Missing Memorable Fact ("Did You Know?")**: Legacy ELH-07 lacked a sourced, memorable carbon footprint fact block.
2. **Missing Visual Identification Question**: Legacy ELH-07 lacked an image-based question evaluating workplace activity data, direct emissions, or unsupported greenwashing claims.
3. **Unclear Activity Data & Data Governance Boundaries**: Legacy content did not explicitly distinguish between raw activity data (litres/kWh) and emission factors, nor did it forbid guessing or inventing data figures.

## Verified Factual Claims & Sources

| Claim | Verified Reality | Source / Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Greenhouse Gas Warming Potentials** | Non-$CO_2$ greenhouse gases (methane, nitrous oxide, fluorinated refrigerants) have warming potentials 28 to 2,000+ times greater than $CO_2$ per kilogram over a 100-year period. | Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) 6th Assessment Report |
| **Scope 1, 2, 3 Boundaries** | Scope 1 covers direct emissions (fuel, refrigerants); Scope 2 covers purchased electricity/cooling; Scope 3 covers value-chain activities (goods, travel, waste). | GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting & Reporting Standard (WRI / WBCSD) |
| **Activity Data vs Emission Factors** | Activity data (litres of diesel, kWh of power) is measured directly; emission factors convert activity data into $CO_2e$ estimates and require authorized sources. | ISO 14064-1 Greenhouse Gases Standard & GHG Protocol Guidance |
