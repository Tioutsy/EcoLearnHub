# ELH-25 — Sustainability for Finance Teams Duplication and Progression Matrix

## Executive Summary

- **Course**: ELH-25 — Sustainability for Finance Teams
- **Target Audience**: Finance Officers, Accountants, Financial Controllers, Accounts Payable/Receivable Staff, and Finance Managers
- **Objective**: Integrate practical sustainability considerations into budgeting, total cost of ownership (TCO) evaluation, expenditure classification, invoice matching, variance tracking, and management reporting without suggesting that finance alone owns environmental performance or technical calculations.
- **Core Progression Distinction**: While ELH-09 introduces broad ESG concepts and ELH-19 reviews operational performance against baselines, **ELH-25 specifically governs financial change control and evidence management** (business case evaluation, TCO lifecycle comparisons, budget coding, vendor invoice verification, and reporting variance disclosure).

## Key Finance Responsibility Boundary Matrix

| Activity | Finance Role | Other Responsible Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Budgeting & Coding** | Create & track dedicated budget lines | Department Manager proposes project budget |
| **TCO Lifecycle Evaluation** | Model purchase, utility & maintenance costs | Engineering / Operations provides technical utility consumption data |
| **Vendor Invoice Matching** | Verify invoice against PO & approved budget | Procurement verifies supplier contract compliance |
| **Technical Claims Review** | Request verification of energy/water savings | Technical Lead / HSE Officer signs off on engineering claims |
| **Ledger Description** | Record exact transaction reality without exaggeration | ESG / Marketing Lead handles external brand communication |
| **Variance Reporting** | Report financial cost overruns/savings | Department Head explains operational causes of variance |
| **Audit Evidence** | Retain financial invoices & PO records | Legal / Compliance defines statutory record retention rules |
