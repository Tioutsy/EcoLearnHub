# ELH-28 — Sustainability for Sales & Marketing Teams Duplication and Progression Matrix

## Executive Summary

- **Course**: ELH-28 — Sustainability for Sales & Marketing Teams
- **Target Audience**: Sales Representatives, Marketing Coordinators, Communications Staff, Business Development Managers, Account Managers, and Customer-Facing Employees
- **Objective**: Communicate sustainability accurately, use approved evidence, avoid misleading green claims, distinguish verified facts from future aspirations, and escalate claims requiring technical or legal review without independently inventing or approving environmental claims.
- **Core Progression Distinction**: While ELH-16 covers general workplace sustainability communication broadly, **ELH-28 specifically governs commercial, promotional, and customer-facing claim controls** (preventing absolute claims like '100% eco-friendly' or 'zero waste', managing material qualifications, tracking evidence sources, verifying certification logo permissions, and executing rapid claim corrections).

## Key Sales & Marketing Responsibility Boundary Matrix

| Activity | Sales & Marketing Role | Other Responsible Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Copy Drafting** | Draft copy from approved claim registers | Technical Data Owner provides verified underlying facts |
| **Claim Scope & Limits** | Add visible qualifications, baselines & dates | Legal / Compliance approves disclaimer placement & logo usage |
| **Customer Questionnaires** | Provide approved facts & escalate unknown details | ESG Lead / Technical Owner verifies technical data answers |
| **Comparative Statements** | Disclose exact baseline dates & parameters | Legal / Compliance approves comparative advertising claims |
| **Certification Logo Use** | Attach logo to approved materials with active license | Certification Body / Legal retains license documentation |
| **Outdated Claim Updates** | Remove or narrow copy promptly when facts change | Operations Lead notifies marketing of operational changes |
| **Claim Register Maintenance** | Maintain central repository of approved wording | Senior Management approves corporate sustainability commitments |
