# ELH-26 — Sustainability for Procurement and Purchasing Teams
## Duplication and Progression Matrix — Sprint 9O

---

## Purpose

This matrix documents how ELH-26 relates to existing and adjacent EcoLearnHub courses — establishing what ELH-26 may briefly reinforce, what it must not reteach, and what distinct capability it introduces.

---

## ELH-26 Distinct Capability

ELH-26 teaches procurement officers, purchasing staff, buyers and department managers how to apply sustainability thinking to **real purchasing decisions** — verifying operational needs, writing specific criteria, comparing whole-life value, evaluating supplier evidence, documenting decisions, managing conflicts of interest, and reviewing supplier performance after award.

**Workplace Output**: A procurement officer or buyer who completes ELH-26 can write a defensible specification, evaluate supplier responses against stated criteria, identify when environmental claims require clarification or escalation, record a reviewable decision note, and monitor a supplier's delivery of agreed requirements.

## Summary: Duplication Risk Assessment

| Course Pair | Duplication Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| ELH-05 vs ELH-26 | **Low** — ELH-05 introduces; ELH-26 applies | ELH-26 explicitly builds on ELH-05 without repeating foundational definitions |
| ELH-25 vs ELH-26 | **Moderate** — Both address TCO and evidence quality | ELH-25 = financial controls; ELH-26 = procurement specification and evaluation. Course content distinguishes the perspectives explicitly |
| ELH-27 vs ELH-26 | **Low** — ELH-26 is general; ELH-27 is domain-specific | ELH-27 applies ELH-26 principles to facilities — progression, not duplication |
| All other listed courses | **Negligible** | ELH-26 is procurement-specific; others are domain-specific or foundational |
