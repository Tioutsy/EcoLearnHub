# ELH-27 — Sustainability for Facilities & Property Teams Duplication and Progression Matrix

## Executive Summary

- **Course**: ELH-27 — Sustainability for Facilities & Property Teams
- **Target Audience**: Facilities Officers, Property Managers, Maintenance Coordinators, Site Supervisors, Building Administrators, Hotel Engineering Support Employees, and Common-Area Estate Staff
- **Objective**: Manage practical workplace sustainability through structured site inspections, preventive maintenance coordination, operational controls, contractor oversight, evidence capture, and escalation without replacing qualified engineers, electricians, plumbers, legal advisers, or fire-safety professionals.
- **Core Progression Distinction**: While ELH-03/04 cover energy/water conservation awareness and ELH-18 collects physical data, **ELH-27 specifically governs physical site operations and contractor completion evidence** (structured site walks, defect logging with photos, temporary interim controls, contractor service reports, post-repair meter verification, and maintenance record retention).

## Key Facilities Responsibility Boundary Matrix

| Activity | Facilities / Property Role | Other Responsible Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Site Inspections** | Conduct daily walks, log defects & photos | HSE / Security supports hazard identification |
| **Temporary Controls** | Apply authorized interim controls (valve isolation, signage) | Qualified Technician performs permanent mechanical repair |
| **High-Voltage / Gas Repair** | Provide safe site access & monitor contractor safety | Licensed Electrician / Gas Specialist executes technical work |
| **Contractor Oversight** | Verify scope, collect service report & photo proof | Procurement manages vendor contract terms |
| **Utility Anomaly Tracking** | Log daily meters & compare vs occupancy trends | Finance evaluates monthly financial utility bill impact |
| **Ticket Closure** | Inspect physical repair & post-repair meter check | Department Lead approves operational closure |
| **Statutory Permits** | Retain inspection logs & contractor service reports | Legal / Compliance interprets statutory permit rules |
