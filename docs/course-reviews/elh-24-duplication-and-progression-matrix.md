# ELH-24 — Sustainability for HR Teams Duplication and Progression Matrix

## Executive Summary

- **Course**: ELH-24 — Sustainability for Human Resources Teams
- **Target Audience**: HR Managers, HR Officers, People Leads, and Department Managers
- **Objective**: Integrate practical sustainability responsibilities, role-based learning pathways, accessible onboarding, voluntary engagement, and audit-ready training evidence into HR processes without presenting HR as the sole owner of technical environmental controls.
- **Core Progression Distinction**: While ELH-20 establishes overall organization accountability and ELH-21 builds general employee participation, **ELH-24 specifically governs HR employee lifecycle integration** (workforce planning, recruitment claims, week-one onboarding, role-based learning assignment, shift accessibility, privacy protection, and training evidence logs).

## Key HR Responsibility Boundary Matrix

| Activity | HR Role | Other Responsible Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sustainability Onboarding** | Coordinate delivery & log completion | Manager / ESG Lead approves content |
| **Technical Environmental SOPs** | Support role documentation | Operations / Facilities owns technical SOPs |
| **Training Assignment** | Administer role-based pathways & log status | Department Manager confirms role relevance |
| **Environmental Claims** | Ensure recruitment copy matches evidence | Approved Technical / Marketing Lead verifies claims |
