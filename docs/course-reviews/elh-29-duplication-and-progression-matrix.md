# ELH-29 — Sustainability for Operations and Frontline Teams
## Duplication and Progression Matrix — Sprint 9P

---

## Purpose

This matrix documents how ELH-29 relates to existing and adjacent EcoLearnHub courses — establishing what ELH-29 may briefly reinforce, what it must not reteach, and what distinct capability it introduces.

---

## ELH-29 Distinct Capability

ELH-29 teaches frontline employees, operations supervisors, and shift teams how to translate approved sustainability requirements into dependable everyday operational practice:
- Following Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) consistently.
- Identifying environmental risks and abnormal conditions during routine work.
- Applying authorised controls at the point of work (e.g., temporary spill socks, drip trays, equipment shutdowns).
- Preventing avoidable micro-waste (compressed air leaks, rinse tap trickles, unsegregated waste).
- Recording deviations, incidents, and observations with specific facts.
- Escalating issues beyond role authority responsibly.
- Executing clear verbal and written shift handovers.
- Balancing sustainability with health, safety, hygiene, quality, and service continuity.

**Workplace Output**: A frontline worker or supervisor who completes ELH-29 can execute SOPs with resource efficiency, recognise abnormal leaks or defects, apply immediate authorised containment, log actionable maintenance tickets, and hand over active issues to incoming shifts cleanly.
