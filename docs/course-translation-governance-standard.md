# Elevio Course Translation Governance & Quality Standard

## Overview
This standard defines the quality, terminology, and assessment integrity guidelines for all bilingual educational content on the Elevio platform (`ELH-01` through `ELH-29`).

---

## 1. Quality & Style Principles
1. **Professional B2B Tone**: Translations must use natural, professional French suitable for Mauritian workplace environments.
2. **Contextual Accuracy**: Avoid literal word-for-word machine translations. Terms like "waste stream", "carbon footprint", "Scope 1", and "due diligence" must follow approved Mauritian B2B conventions.
3. **Inclusive & Clear**: Language must be approachable across varying educational backgrounds without sacrificing technical accuracy.

---

## 2. Assessment & Quiz Integrity Standards
1. **Identical Question Stems**: French quiz questions must test the exact same learning objective as their English counterparts.
2. **Balanced Distractors**: Answer choices must remain equally plausible, ensuring grammar or sentence length does not hint at the correct option.
3. **Identical Scoring & Thresholds**: Pass thresholds remain fixed at 80% across all locales.
4. **Option Feedback Parity**: Both correct and incorrect option feedback must provide clear, constructive workplace explanations in French.
