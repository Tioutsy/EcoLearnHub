# Learner Getting Started Guide — EcoLearnHub

Welcome to EcoLearnHub! This guide explains how to activate your account, navigate your learning dashboard, complete assigned training courses, and track your certificates.

---

## 1. Accepting Your Invitation & Activating Access
1. Open the invitation email sent by your organisation's administrator.
2. Click **Accept Invitation** or navigate to the activation link.
3. Sign in or register your account.
4. Your account will automatically activate and link to your company workspace.
5. You will be directed to your primary next learning action (e.g. required overdue training, due-soon course, or starting module).

---

## 2. Learner Dashboard Navigation
Your home dashboard prioritizes your active learning tasks:
- **Primary Action**: Displays the single most important task (e.g. *Start Course*, *Continue Course*, *Retry Quiz*, or *View Certificate*).
- **Required & Due Soon Training**: Shows assigned compliance courses sorted by urgency and due date.
- **In Progress & Resume**: Pick up right where you left off across any device.
- **Learning Pathways**: Follow structured course series with clear progress indicators.
- **Recommended & Catalogue**: Explore optional courses included in your company's subscription plan.

---

## 3. Completing Courses & Quizzes
- Progress is automatically saved as you complete lessons and interactive modules.
- End-of-course quizzes require a passing score (typically 80%).
- If you fail a quiz attempt, review the explanation feedback and click **Retry Quiz** when ready.
- Upon passing, your official certificate is instantly generated and stored in your profile.

---

## 4. Notification Preferences
- **Operational Notifications** (Required training assignments, due date reminders, account updates) cannot be disabled.
- **Optional Engagement Notifications** (Recommended course suggestions, pathway encouragement) can be customized in your profile settings.
