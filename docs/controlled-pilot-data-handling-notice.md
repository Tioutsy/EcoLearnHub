# Controlled Pilot Data Handling & Privacy Notice — EcoLearnHub

This notice outlines how personal and corporate learning data is handled during EcoLearnHub's controlled commercial pilot.

---

## 1. Data Categories Handled
- **User Identity**: Name, work email address, department, job role.
- **Learning Activity**: Course enrolments, lesson progress, quiz attempts, completion timestamps, certificates, and badges.
- **Workplace Actions**: Self-reported sustainability commitments and reflections.

## 2. Tenant Isolation & Protection
- Data is strictly partitioned by `company_id`.
- Cross-company data access is blocked by server-side authorization controls.
- Spreadsheet exports sanitize formula injection characters (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`).

## 3. Post-Pilot Data Retention
- Formal training completion evidence and certificates are preserved for company audit compliance.
- Unconverted company access may be paused or archived upon company request.
