## 1. Data Controller & Processor Roles
EcoLearnHub operates as a Data Processor on behalf of customer companies (Data Controllers) regarding corporate employee training data.

## 2. Information Collected
- **Account Data**: Name, work email address, department, job title, company membership.
- **Learning Data**: Course progress, lesson completions, quiz scores, certificate records.
- **Action Data**: Workplace sustainability commitments and self-reported reflections.

## 3. Data Protection & Security
- All customer data is stored in isolated PostgreSQL database structures.
- Sensitive access keys and passwords are encrypted or hashed.
- Export files protect against spreadsheet formula injection.
