# Manager Scope Standard

## 1. Overview
This standard defines the operational scope for **Managers** (`manager`) within Elevio Skills.

---

## 2. Manager Scope Rules
1. **Single-Tenant Bound**: A Manager belongs strictly to one company (`companyId`).
2. **Team & Department Scope**: A Manager can view and assign courses to employees belonging to their assigned department or team.
3. **No Administrative Rights**: A Manager **cannot**:
   - Add, edit, or delete employees.
   - Access company subscription or billing settings.
   - Modify organisation profiles or global platform settings.
4. **Permitted Actions**:
   - View team learning progress and completion statistics.
   - Assign training courses to team members.
   - Review workplace action commitments submitted by team members.
   - Download team training completion evidence (CSV/PDF).
