# Controlled Pilot Programme — EcoLearnHub Sprint 8G

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the programme structure, duration, cohort size, sector targets, and learning pathways for EcoLearnHub's controlled commercial pilot.

---

## 1. Pilot Scope & Duration

- **Cohort Size**: 2–5 pilot companies, 20–100 learners in total.
- **Duration**: 4–6 weeks per company (1 week prep, 3–4 weeks active learning, 1 week evaluation).
- **Target Sectors**: Hospitality, Facilities Management, Professional Services, Manufacturing/Logistics, Retail.
- **Pathway Scope**: Foundation course (ELH-01), practical workplace course, role-relevant module, and workplace action commitment.
