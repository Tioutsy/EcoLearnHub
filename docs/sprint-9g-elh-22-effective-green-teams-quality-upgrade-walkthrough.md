3. **13-Part Applied Course Progression Implemented**:
   - **Opening Hook**: A multi-site Mauritian enterprise launches a Green Team comprising head-office HR, marketing, and admin volunteers. After 3 months: meetings clash with operational shifts; Housekeeping, Warehouse, Maintenance, and Night-shift staff are omitted; 15 ideas are discussed but zero owners are named; budget requests sit unapproved for months; meeting frequency (12 meetings held!) is cited as proof of success; one enthusiastic employee carries all files. Members disengage.
   - **Why Effective Green Teams Matter**: Cross-departmental insights, early problem identification, structured recommendations to management, continuity between campaigns, and practical follow-through.
   - **Sourced Memorable Fact ("Did You Know?")**: Sourced block from ISO 14001:2015 Clause 5.3 & ISO 9001:2015 Clause 5.3 (*Organizational roles, responsibilities and authorities*) on clear mandate, operational representation, and structured feedback loops without making unsupported productivity claims.
   - **The TEAMWORK Operational Framework**: 8-step operational framework (**T**arget a clear purpose, **E**nsure relevant representation, **A**ssign authority & accountability, **M**ake meetings useful, **W**ork from evidence & reality, **O**rganise actions & resources, **R**eview delivery & remove blockers, **K**eep team relevant & sustainable).
   - **Visual Identification Question**: Integrated realistic high-resolution photograph of a Mauritian commercial workplace meeting room (`visual-sustainability-green-team-effectiveness.png`) displaying highlighted team defects (head-office staff bias, empty seat labeled 'Night Shift & Frontline Omitted', unassigned actions, long pending budget approvals, meeting count vanity metrics, single person holding all action folders, no written mandate, meeting time clashing with operational shift, unanswered suggestion overflow).
   - **Worked Mauritian Workplace Scenario**: Grand Baie resort green team delivery log across Housekeeping, Kitchen, Front Office, Maintenance, Security, Procurement, HR, Finance, and Night Shift with clear decision outcomes (Approved, Pilot, Escalated, Referred).
   - **Applied Decision Scenario**: Manufacturing green team meeting monthly for 6 months with 12 ideas discussed, 2 completed, 5 with no owners, 3 awaiting approval, operations rep missing due to dispatch clash, finance getting uncosted requests (reviewing mandate, representation, decision flow, action ownership before launching another campaign).
   - **Role-Based Micro-Decisions**: Micro-decisions across 12 workplace roles.
   - **10 Scenario Quiz Questions**: Full answer explanations for all correct and incorrect options.
   - **Learner Commitment & Disclaimer**: Practical team operating commitments and practical disclaimer ("Practical workplace guidance; not legal advice, employee-relations certification, environmental assurance, management-system certification, or independent verification of an organization's environmental performance or workplace culture").
