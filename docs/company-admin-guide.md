# Company Administrator Guide — EcoLearnHub Workspace Operations

Welcome to the EcoLearnHub Company Administrator Workspace. This guide details how to manage your team, create departments, assign training courses and learning pathways, monitor completion deadlines, and maintain audit-ready records.

---

## 1. Dashboard & Administrative Overview
Log in as a Company Administrator and navigate to the **Company Workspace Dashboard**.
- **Subscription & Seat Capacity**: Monitor your total seat allowance, active members, and remaining available capacity.
- **Employee Status**: Track active employees, pending invitations, and deactivated team members.
- **Training Health**: View assigned courses, in-progress modules, completions, and overdue training alerts.
- **Onboarding Readiness**: Review recommendations to keep your workspace setup complete.

---

## 2. Employee Directory & Member Operations

### Adding & Inviting Employees
1. Go to **Employees & Team**.
2. **Individual Invitation**: Click **Add Employee**, enter name, email, department, and role (`Learner`, `Manager`, `Company Administrator`).
3. **Bulk CSV Import**: Click **Import CSV**, download the template, populate employee details, and upload. Review row validations before confirming.

### Managing Member Lifecycle
- **Resend Invitation**: Click **Resend Invitation** next to a pending member to generate a fresh single-use activation link.
- **Revoke Invitation**: Click **Revoke Invitation** to cancel pending access before an employee activates their account.
- **Deactivate Employee**: When an employee leaves your organisation, select **Deactivate**. This frees up a seat in your subscription band while **fully preserving** all past training completions, certificates, and quiz logs for audit compliance.
- **Reactivate Employee**: Select **Reactivate** to restore access for a returning employee if seat capacity permits.

---

## 3. Department & Manager Scope Setup
1. Go to **Departments & Teams**.
2. **Create Department**: Enter department name (e.g. *Operations*, *Finance*, *Sustainability*) and optional department code.
3. **Assign Department Manager**: Designate a team manager. Managers gain scoped visibility to monitor training progress for employees in their designated department.
4. **Archive Department**: Archive unused departments without deleting historical training records.

---

## 4. Training Assignments & Learning Pathways

### Assigning Training
1. Go to **Course & Pathway Assignments** and select **Assign Training**.
2. Choose **Single Course** (e.g. *ELH-01 Sustainability Foundations*) or a **Learning Pathway** (e.g. *Core Sustainability Pathway*).
3. Target **Individual Employees**, a **Department**, or **All Active Employees**.
4. Set optional **Due Date** for compliance tracking.
5. Select assignment type: **Required** or **Recommended**.
6. Review entitlement and prerequisite eligibility before confirming assignment.

### Managing Active Assignments
- Filter assignments by employee, course, department, status, or due date.
- Update due dates or cancel unstarted assignments as operational needs change.
