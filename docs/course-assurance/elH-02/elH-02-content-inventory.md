## 3. Detailed Item Inventory

### Metadata & Overview
- **Title**: Waste Sorting & the Mauritian Bin System
- **Description**: Help employees identify common workplace waste, make better sorting decisions, reduce contamination, and understand that recycling rules depend on your employer's designated waste collector.
- **Learning Objectives**:
  1. Identify common workplace waste categories and clean recyclable materials.
  2. Separate clean, accepted materials from contaminated or unsuitable items.
  3. Follow workplace-specific bin labels and approved collector instructions.
  4. Recognise special waste (like batteries and e-waste) that requires escalation.
  5. Take practical action to reduce sorting mistakes and avoid landfill contamination.
