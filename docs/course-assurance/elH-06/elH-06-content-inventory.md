## 3. Detailed Item Inventory

### Metadata & Overview
- **Title**: Green Office Practices
- **Description**: Integrate everyday office routines, shared workspace management, printing and digital boundaries, meeting efficiency, and respectful team habits into a sustainable workplace culture.
- **Learning Objectives**:
  1. Recognise common resource waste across a normal office working day.
  2. Apply practical sustainability habits in personal and shared workspaces.
  3. Apply sensible printing and digital document boundaries while protecting data confidentiality.
  4. Plan efficient, lower-waste meetings and shared refreshment routines.
  5. Distinguish direct employee actions from changes requiring site procedure or facilities escalation.
  6. Escalate office faults and safety issues constructively without policing or blaming colleagues.
  7. Select one practical workplace commitment to support a green office culture.
