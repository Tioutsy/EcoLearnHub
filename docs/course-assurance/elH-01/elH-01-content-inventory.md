## 3. Detailed Item Inventory

### Metadata & Overview
- **Title**: Sustainability Foundations
- **Description**: A short, practical introduction to workplace sustainability. In about 20 minutes, you will learn what sustainability really means, why it matters for Mauritius, and the simple actions you can take at work every day.
- **Learning Objectives**:
  1. Understand what sustainability means in everyday terms.
  2. See why sustainability matters for Mauritius and the workplace.
  3. Choose practical actions around waste, water, and energy.
  4. Make a personal workplace sustainability commitment.
