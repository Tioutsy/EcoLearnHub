## 3. Detailed Item Inventory

### Metadata & Overview
- **Title**: Energy Efficiency at Work
- **Description**: Help employees identify avoidable workplace energy waste, optimize air-conditioning and lighting habits, follow end-of-day shutdown procedures, and escalate technical issues safely.
- **Learning Objectives**:
  1. Recognise common sources of avoidable workplace energy waste in Mauritian facilities.
  2. Distinguish between direct employee actions, site-procedure checks, and technical escalation.
  3. Apply appropriate energy-saving practices without affecting safety, comfort, or critical operations.
  4. Respond safely and effectively to realistic workplace energy-waste situations.
  5. Choose one practical energy-efficiency commitment relevant to your daily role.
