# ELH-10 Legal Claim Register

## 1. Executive Summary
This register documents every legal or mandatory compliance statement in **ELH-10 — Environmental Compliance**, classifying each statement according to the Legal Hierarchy (Legal Requirement, Permit Condition, Company Procedure, or Good Practice) to ensure statutory laws are not confused with internal guidelines or voluntary practices.
