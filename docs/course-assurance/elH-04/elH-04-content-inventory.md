## 3. Detailed Item Inventory

### Metadata & Overview
- **Title**: Water Conservation
- **Description**: Learn how daily workplace habits, early leak reporting, and sensible conservation boundaries eliminate water waste without compromising hygiene, safety, or operational standards.
- **Learning Objectives**:
  1. Explain why responsible water use matters to employees, businesses, and the Mauritian environment.
  2. Identify common forms of avoidable water waste in commercial and service workplaces.
  3. Take safe, practical actions to reduce water waste within employee authority.
  4. Distinguish between direct actions, site-procedure checks, and issues requiring technical escalation.
  5. Respond appropriately to realistic workplace water-use and leak scenarios.
  6. Select one practical workplace commitment to support responsible water stewardship.
