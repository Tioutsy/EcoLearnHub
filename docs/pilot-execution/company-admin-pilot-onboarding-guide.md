# Company Administrator Pilot Onboarding Guide (Sprint 10E)

## Executive Summary
This guide supports Company Administrators in managing employee rosters, assigning training pathways, and reviewing progress during the pilot.

---

## Administrator Steps

1. **Sign In & Accept Pilot Notice**: Authenticate via invitation link and acknowledge `company_pilot_notice` (v1.0).
2. **Employee Roster Intake**: Add employees individually or upload CSV file containing Email, Name, Department, and Job Title.
3. **Training Pathway Review**: Verify assigned micro-learning modules (available in English and French).
4. **Progress Monitoring**: View live progress metrics and completion rates at `/company/reports`.
5. **Support Form**: Submit any questions or technical issues directly via `/support`.
