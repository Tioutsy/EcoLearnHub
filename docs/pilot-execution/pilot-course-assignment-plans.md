# Pilot Course Assignment Plans (Sprint 10C)

## Executive Summary
This document defines department-specific course assignment pathways for pilot companies to ensure learners receive relevant, actionable micro-learning.

---

## Sector Pathway Templates

### 1. Hospitality Sector Pathway (e.g. Lux Resorts)
- **General Staff (Front Office, Housekeeping, F&B)**:
  - Course 1: `ELH-01` — Sustainability Foundations
  - Course 2: `ELH-02` — Waste Sorting & Mauritian Bins
  - Course 3: `ELH-04` — Water Conservation
- **Facilities & Maintenance**:
  - Course 1: `ELH-01` — Sustainability Foundations
  - Course 2: `ELH-03` — Energy Efficiency at Work
  - Course 3: `ELH-27` — Sustainability for Facilities & Property Teams
- **Procurement & Purchasing**:
  - Course 1: `ELH-01` — Sustainability Foundations
  - Course 2: `ELH-05` — Sustainable Procurement
  - Course 3: `ELH-26` — Sustainability for Procurement Teams

### 2. Financial & Professional Services Pathway (e.g. MCB)
- **General Corporate Staff**:
  - Course 1: `ELH-01` — Sustainability Foundations
  - Course 2: `ELH-06` — Green Office Practices
  - Course 3: `ELH-07` — Carbon Footprint Awareness
- **Finance & Accounting**:
  - Course 1: `ELH-01` — Sustainability Foundations
  - Course 2: `ELH-09` — ESG Fundamentals
  - Course 3: `ELH-25` — Sustainability for Finance Teams
- **Management & Leadership**:
  - Course 1: `ELH-01` — Sustainability Foundations
  - Course 2: `ELH-14` — Departmental Sustainability Goals
  - Course 3: `ELH-20` — Roles & Accountability
