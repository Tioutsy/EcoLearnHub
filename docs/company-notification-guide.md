# Company Notification & Communication Guide — EcoLearnHub

This guide details EcoLearnHub's notification system for company administrators, managers, and learners.

---

## 1. Notification Classifications

### Operational Notifications (Mandatory)
These messages are essential for security, access, and compliance tracking. They cannot be turned off by learners:
- Account invitations and activation links
- Training course assignment notices
- Material due-date changes
- Course completion & certificate confirmations

### Compliance Training Reminders
Policy-driven reminders scheduled by your organisation:
- Pending invitation reminders (3 days & 7 days)
- Assigned course not started
- Upcoming due date alerts (7 days & 2 days prior)
- Overdue assignment alerts

### Optional Engagement Messages
Optional learning recommendations and progress suggestions. Learners can manage these in their account preferences:
- Inactive course resume suggestions
- Pathway continuation prompts
- Quiz retry encouragement

---

## 2. Company Admin Visibility & Delivery Statuses

Company administrators can inspect delivery records under **Workspace Settings > Notification Logs**:
- **Pending**: Message queued for delivery.
- **Processing**: Claimed by delivery worker.
- **Delivered**: Provider accepted message for delivery.
- **Failed**: Delivery failed (e.g. invalid email address or network error).
- **Skipped**: Message omitted due to deduplication, user opt-out, or completed assignment.

---

## 3. Manager Manual Reminders
- Managers can dispatch approved 1-click reminders for employees within their designated department scope.
- Manual reminders are rate-limited, audited, and deduplicated.
