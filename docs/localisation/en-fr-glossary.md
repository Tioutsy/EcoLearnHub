# EN / FR B2B Sustainability Glossary

## 1. Overview
This glossary establishes standard English-to-French terminology for Elevio Skills within the Mauritian corporate sustainability and ESG training context.

## 3. Style & Wording Guidelines
1. **Professional B2B Tone**: Avoid informal phrasing (`Tu`). Use polite, professional imperative and second-person formal form (`Vous`).
2. **Mauritian Context Sensitivity**: Respect local terminology for regulations (e.g. `Ministère de l'Environnement`, `Code du Travail`).
3. **No Concatenated Sentences**: Translate complete sentences to preserve natural French word order rather than appending fragments.
