# Pilot Company Onboarding Checklist — EcoLearnHub

This checklist guides the step-by-step onboarding of pilot companies onto EcoLearnHub.

---

## Onboarding Steps

1. **Company Authorization**: Confirm pilot approval and employee band limit.
2. **Profile Completion**: Update organisation details, industry sector, and primary contact.
3. **Department Setup**: Create company departments (e.g., Facilities, Operations, ESG).
4. **Employee Import**: Upload validated employee CSV file (emails, names, roles, departments).
5. **Initial Course Assignment**: Assign starting course (ELH-01 Sustainability Foundations) to employees.
6. **Invitation Dispatch**: Release activation emails to imported employees.
7. **Engagement Monitoring**: Track activation rates and dispatch reminders after 3 days.
