# Pilot Case Study and Testimonial Standard (Sprint 10B)

## Executive Summary
This document defines the opt-in governance controls for using pilot company feedback, case studies, logos, or quotes.

---

## Marketing & Case Study Guidelines

1. **Explicit Opt-In Required**: Pilot company logos, administrator quotes, or named case studies will NOT be published without prior written approval from the pilot company's authorized representative.
2. **Default Anonymised Reporting**: By default, pilot findings used in public collateral are reported in aggregate anonymised form (e.g. *"A 50-employee hospitality pilot achieved an 88% completion rate within 14 days"*).
3. **No Inflated Environmental Claims**: Public case studies avoid claiming unverified carbon, water, or waste reduction figures, focusing strictly on employee participation, learning pass rates, and verified workplace action submissions.
4. **Withdrawal Right**: Pilot companies reserve the right to request removal or revision of any unpublished draft case study at any time.
