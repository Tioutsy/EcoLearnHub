# Company Training Analytics & Intervention Guide — EcoLearnHub

This guide details how company administrators and supervisors use EcoLearnHub's analytics workspace, intervention queue, and behaviour-change tracking.

---

## 1. Analytics Domains & Dashboards

Access your company analytics dashboard at **Workspace Settings > Training Analytics**:

- **Participation Funnel**: Tracks user progression from invited -> activated -> assigned -> started -> completed -> pathway finished.
- **Completion Rates & On-Time Performance**: Evaluates overall assignment completion and deadline adherence.
- **Department Comparisons**: Compares completion metrics across company departments (with minimum sample size safeguards for small teams).
- **Course & Assessment Health**: Examines pass rates, average quiz scores, and retry rates to pinpoint content difficulty or knowledge gaps.

---

## 2. Manager Intervention Workspace

Managers can access their prioritized intervention queue at **Workspace Settings > Manager Interventions**:

- **Priority Queue**: Highlights learners needing assistance (e.g. pending invitations > 3 days, overdue courses, stalled pathways, quiz retries).
- **Supported Interventions**: 1-click reminders, due date extensions, course reassignments, and manager check-in logging.
- **Bulk Operations**: Perform safe, multi-learner reminder or extension actions with automatic tenant isolation and deduplication.

---

## 3. Workplace Action Commitments

EcoLearnHub connects course completion to workplace action:
- **Intention (Level 3)**: Completers choose a practical sustainability commitment.
- **Self-Reported Action (Level 4)**: Employees mark action completion and record reflections.
- **Manager Confirmation (Level 5)**: Supervisors confirm high-impact workplace actions.
