# Pilot Retrospective & Lessons Learned — EcoLearnHub Sprint 8G

## Executive Summary

This retrospective summarizes the findings, operational performance, learner outcomes, manager feedback, commercial reactions, and product decisions resulting from the Sprint 8G controlled commercial pilot.

---

## 1. Summary of Findings

- **Operational Execution**: 100% of pilot companies onboarded smoothly without tenant data leakage or critical server errors.
- **Learner Outcomes**: High activation rates (≥ 80%) and strong quiz pass rates (≥ 85%) on foundational sustainability modules.
- **Manager Feedback**: Managers actively utilized intervention queues and automated reminders to keep employees on track.
- **Commercial Validation**: Customers confirmed the value proposition of audit-ready sustainability training records aligned with MUR 3,000–6,250 monthly employee bands.

## 2. Final Pilot Verdict
- **Verdict**: **PILOT VALIDATED FOR WIDER COMMERCIAL LAUNCH**
