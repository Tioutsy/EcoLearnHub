# Pilot Discovery Framework (Sprint 10F)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the structured discovery record connected to each pilot candidate organisation.

---

## Discovery Information Capture

- **Organisation Context**: Sector, total workforce, locations, key operational departments.
- **Buyer & Sponsor Context**: HR lead, Sustainability officer, IT contact, procurement involvement.
- **Training Needs & Gaps**: Current ESG priorities, key knowledge gaps, mandatory vs optional courses.
- **Pilot Expectations**: Target learner count, proposed duration (4 weeks), reporting expectations.
- **Commercial Context**: Expected monthly employee band post-pilot (Band 1 to Band 5).
