# Pilot Candidate Qualification Standard (Sprint 10F)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the candidate qualification framework, criteria, and qualification evaluation rules.

---

## 12 Qualification Criteria

1. **Genuine External Entity**: Candidate company is a registered commercial organisation.
2. **Authorised Decision-Maker Contact**: Identified primary contact with authority to confirm pilot participation.
3. **Target Learner Cohort**: Clear employee count (10 to 500+) and proposed pilot learner group (10 to 50).
4. **Defined Workplace Training Need**: Identified sustainability micro-learning requirements.
5. **Catalogue Alignment**: Current catalogue (ELH-01 to ELH-29) satisfies identified training needs.
6. **Timeframe Commitment**: Ability to complete 4-week active learning pilot.
7. **Feedback Willingness**: Commitment to participate in entry, midpoint, and completion surveys.
8. **Evidence Intake Consent**: Agreement to provide written participation confirmation.
9. **Data Privacy Alignment**: Compliance with Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 boundaries.
10. **Legal Claim Disclaimer**: Acknowledgment that Elevio Skills makes no unapproved legal or HRDC refund claims.
11. **Feature Alignment**: Training requirements fit within current platform capabilities.
12. **Commercial Value**: Potential post-pilot conversion into monthly employee subscription band.
