# Pilot Outreach and Follow-Up Standard (Sprint 10F)

## Executive Summary
This document establishes restrained outreach controls and no-contact safeguards for candidate communications.

---

## Outreach Safeguards

- **Bulk Email Prohibition**: No automated unsolicited mass email campaigns.
- **Same-Day Duplicate Block**: Same-day duplicate follow-ups to the same contact are blocked.
- **No-Contact Respect**: Candidates in `DECLINED` or `WITHDRAWN` status are excluded from outreach.
- **Founder-Led BD**: All candidate communications represent targeted, professional founder outreach.
