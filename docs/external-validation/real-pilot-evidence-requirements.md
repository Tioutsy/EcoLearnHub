# Real Pilot Evidence Requirements (Sprint 10D)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the minimum evidence required across participation, operations, feedback, and commercial categories before a pilot can be classified as validated.

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

1. **Participation Evidence**: Signed pilot agreement or written email confirmation from an authorized company officer, specifying company legal name, primary contact email, learner cap, and pilot start/end dates.
2. **Operational Evidence**: System-generated records of employee invitations sent, accounts activated, course starts, quiz attempts, and certificates issued.
3. **Feedback Evidence**: Completed survey responses from learners, managers, and pilot administrators.
4. **Commercial Evidence**: Documented decision-maker feedback, continuation preference, employee band selection, and formal procurement requirements.
