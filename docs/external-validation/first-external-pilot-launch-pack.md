# First External Pilot Launch Pack (Sprint 10D)

## Executive Summary
This pack provides company-facing materials and internal launch procedures for the first live external pilot.

---

## 1. Company-Facing Launch Materials

- **Pilot Overview**: 4-week micro-learning pilot for workplace sustainability (ELH-01 to ELH-29 in EN/FR).
- **Learner Expectations**: 15–20 minutes per course module; 80% pass score for verified certificate.
- **Privacy & Visibility**: Training progress and certificates visible to company admin; no sensitive medical/financial data requested.
- **Support Route**: Direct in-platform support request form (`/support`).

---

## 2. Internal Operations Checklists

- **Daily Launch Week Monitoring**: Inspect activation rates, invitation errors, and course starts.
- **Weekly Progress Review**: Review completion rates, average scores, and open support tickets.
- **Final Pilot Report**: Export company evaluation report (`GET /api/pilots/company-report`).
