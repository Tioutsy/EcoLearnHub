# Pilot Participation Evidence Standard (Sprint 10E)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the participation evidence intake workflow, acceptable evidence types, and review statuses required before a pilot can be activated.

---

## Evidence Review Statuses

1. `NO_EVIDENCE`: No participation confirmation submitted.
2. `EVIDENCE_SUBMITTED`: Confirmation document or email reference uploaded by candidate administrator.
3. `UNDER_REVIEW`: Platform Admin inspecting confirmation details.
4. `ACCEPTED`: Platform Admin formally approves participation evidence; candidate becomes eligible for `ACTIVATION_READY`.
5. `REJECTED`: Participation evidence rejected due to missing authorised signature or incomplete scope.
6. `EXPIRED`: Participation terms window lapsed without activation.

---

## Allowed Evidence Formats
- Signed PDF Participation Agreement.
- Authorised Corporate Email Confirmation (`.eml` or `.pdf` scan).
- Verified In-Platform Terms Acceptance Record.
