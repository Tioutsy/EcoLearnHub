# Sprint 10F Evidence Eligibility Review

## Executive Summary
This document records the evidence eligibility review confirming which acquisition milestones qualify as external market validation.

---

## Evidence Eligibility Matrix

- **Draft / Issued Proposal**: Pre-activation milestone; does NOT constitute external market validation.
- **Accepted Proposal & Participation Confirmation**: Eligible for Stage 4 (`stage_4_pilot_participation_confirmed`).
- **Guarded Activation**: Eligible for Stage 5 (`stage_5_pilot_launched`).
- **4-Week Operational Completion & Survey Feedback**: Eligible for Stage 6 (`stage_6_pilot_completed`).
