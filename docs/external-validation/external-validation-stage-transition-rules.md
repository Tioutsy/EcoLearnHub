# External Validation Stage Transition Rules (Sprint 10E)

## Executive Summary
This document establishes stage transition rules for Stage 0 (Internal Technical Validation) through Stage 8 (Commercial Customer Confirmed).

---

## Stage Transition Matrix

- **Stage 0 (Internal Technical Validation)** -> **Stage 4 (Pilot Participation Confirmed)**: Requires accepted participation evidence from a non-test corporate entity.
- **Stage 4 (Participation Confirmed)** -> **Stage 5 (Pilot Launched)**: Requires successful execution of guarded activation workflow and at least 1 real learner invitation.
- **Stage 5 (Pilot Launched)** -> **Stage 6 (Pilot Completed)**: Requires completion of 4-week active learning period and generated company pilot report.
- **Stage 6 (Pilot Completed)** -> **Stage 8 (Commercial Customer Confirmed)**: Requires signed commercial contract and approved subscription band.
