# Customer Health Score Standard (Sprint 10O)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the customer-health scoring methodology across activation, engagement, completion, support, and reliability dimensions.

---

## 1. Health Score Formula
- **Dimensions**: Activation (20%), Completion (30%), Manager Adoption (20%), Support SLA (15%), Reliability (15%).
- **Health Bands**: `HEALTHY` (>= 85%), `WATCH` (70-84%), `AT RISK` (< 70%).
