# Customer Success State Standard (Sprint 10O)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the customer-success state machine and transition rules.

---

## 1. Customer Success Lifecycle States

```text
ACTIVATED ──► ONBOARDING_SUPPORT ──► EARLY_USAGE ──► ACTIVE ──► VALUE_CONFIRMED ──► EXPANSION_READY ──► RENEWAL_CONFIRMED
```

- **ACTIVATED -> ACTIVE**: Requires genuine learner login & course activity.
- **ACTIVE -> VALUE_CONFIRMED**: Requires verified operational use & completion.
- **VALUE_CONFIRMED -> EXPANSION_READY**: Requires positive adoption and zero open SEV-1/SEV-2 issues.
