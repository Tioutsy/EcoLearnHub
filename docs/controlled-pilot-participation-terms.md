# Controlled Pilot Participation Terms — EcoLearnHub

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These terms govern corporate participation in the EcoLearnHub controlled commercial pilot program.

---

## 1. Scope of Pilot
- **Duration**: 4 to 6 weeks from company activation.
- **Included Scope**: EcoLearnHub interactive sustainability courses (ELH-01 through ELH-29), company admin workspace, employee import, progress tracking, quiz assessments, certificates, and manager intervention reporting.
- **Support**: Priority developer and onboarding support provided by the EcoLearnHub team.

## 2. Company Responsibilities
- Designate an authorized Company Administrator and primary contact.
- Provide validated employee list for course assignment.
- Share qualitative feedback on platform usability, reporting clarity, and commercial relevance.

## 3. Disclaimers & Legal Notice
- Pilot participation does NOT grant or imply guaranteed HRDC refundability or statutory ESG compliance certification.
- All customer data is maintained strictly isolated per company tenant.
