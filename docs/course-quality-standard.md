# EcoLearnHub Course Quality Standard

## Overview
This standard defines the mandatory instructional design, content structure, visual question guidelines, memorable fact requirements, and release threshold for all EcoLearnHub courses.

---

## 1. Mandatory 13-Part Progression
Every active course must follow the 13-part progression:
1. Course Introduction (Action-based objectives)
2. Opening Hook (Relatable workplace scenario)
3. Why It Matters (3 perspectives: Employee, Organisation, Environment)
4. Essential Knowledge (Plain language concepts)
5. Mauritian Workplace Example (Grounded local context)
6. Practical Workplace Actions (Role-specific actions)
7. Memorable "Did You Know?" Fact (Sourced & memorable)
8. Visual Identification Question (Accessible, mobile-friendly image question)
9. Applied Decision Scenario (Realistic role-based decision)
10. Knowledge Check (Intermediate comprehension checks)
11. Final Quiz (Assessed questions with full explanations)
12. Learner Commitment (Un-graded workplace action commitment)
13. Completion & Next Step (Badge, summary & next course recommendation)

---

## 2. Release Threshold & Quality Rubric
A course must achieve at least **85/100 points** on the course quality rubric and contain **0 release blockers** to be approved for production publication.
