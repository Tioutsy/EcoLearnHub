# Sprint 8K — Course Quality Standard & Catalogue Architecture Audit

## Executive Summary

Sprint 8K inspects EcoLearnHub's course catalogue learning architecture and establishes a mandatory, 100-point course quality rubric, visual media governance policy, and automated diagnostic framework.

---

## 1. Inventory & Learning Architecture Inspection

- **Active Course Inventory**: 29 active courses (`ELH-01` through `ELH-29`).
- **Database Schema**: Supported block types include `text`, `callout`, `scenario`, `quiz`, `commitment`, and `visual_question`.
- **Reference Course Selection**: **ELH-01 — Sustainability Foundations** is selected as the benchmark reference course to demonstrate the new 13-part course quality standard.

---

## 2. Mandatory 13-Part Course Quality Progression

1. **Course Introduction**: Title, duration, level, prerequisites, and action-based learning objectives.
2. **Hook**: Relatable workplace scenario opening (no dictionary definitions).
3. **Why It Matters**: Business, employee, and environmental dimensions.
4. **Essential Knowledge**: Plain language concepts without long academic passages.
5. **Mauritian Workplace Example**: Grounded local operational context (e.g. hotel, office, retail).
6. **Practical Workplace Actions**: Specific, role-based, observable employee actions.
7. **Memorable "Did You Know?" Fact**: Sourced, relevant, non-shocking factual question.
8. **Visual Identification Question**: Accessible, mobile-friendly image question with descriptive alt text and feedback.
9. **Applied Decision Scenario**: Workplace role decision with realistic options and feedback.
10. **Knowledge Check**: Targeted intermediate checks during lessons.
11. **Final Quiz**: Assessed questions covering learning objectives with full explanations.
12. **Learner Commitment**: One actionable, un-graded workplace action choice.
13. **Completion & Next Step**: Professional completion summary, badge, and logical next course recommendation.

---

## 3. Scoring Rubric & Quality Release Threshold

- **Total Score**: 100 points across 15 criteria.
- **Release Threshold**: Minimum **85/100** score with **0 release blockers** (no empty/placeholder content, no unsupported claims, no broken images, no missing alt text, no missing quiz feedback).
