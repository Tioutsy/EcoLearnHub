# Portfolio Customer Health Standard (Sprint 10P)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies portfolio-wide customer health calculation and state classification.

---

## 1. Portfolio Health Bands
- `HEALTHY` (Score >= 85%): Active completion, zero blockers.
- `WATCH` (Score 70–84%): Minor engagement lag.
- `AT_RISK` (Score < 70%): High support burden or low progress.
