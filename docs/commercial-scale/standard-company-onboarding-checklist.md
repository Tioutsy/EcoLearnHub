# Standard Company Onboarding Checklist (Sprint 10P)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the standard 5-step operational onboarding checklist for all commercial clients.

---

## 1. Onboarding Checklist
1. Commercial readiness (proposal accepted, pricing band confirmed).
2. Organisation readiness (company tenant created, admin invited).
3. Data readiness (employee CSV intake, department mapping).
4. Learning readiness (course pathways assigned).
5. Support readiness (CS owner assigned, incident SLA active).
