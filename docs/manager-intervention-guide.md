# Manager Intervention & Support Guide — EcoLearnHub

This guide helps department managers and team leaders use EcoLearnHub's intervention workflows to support employee learning effectively.

---

## 1. Using the Intervention Queue
Your intervention queue prioritizes team members based on actionable training milestones:
- **Pending Activation**: Team members invited over 3 days ago who haven't activated their account.
- **Assigned Not Started**: Assigned courses close to due dates.
- **Overdue Assignments**: Courses past due dates requiring follow-up support or due date adjustments.
- **Quiz Retries Required**: Learners needing review encouragement after a failed attempt.

---

## 2. Choosing Recommended Interventions
- **Send Reminder**: Dispatches a neutral, polite reminder email (deduplicated automatically).
- **Extend Due Date**: Adjusts assignment target dates for team members with high workload or leave.
- **Log Manager Check-In**: Records 1-on-1 offline support provided to the employee.
- **Confirm Workplace Commitment**: Verifies completed workplace sustainability actions.
