# External Pilot Onboarding Pack (Sprint 10B)

## Executive Summary
This pack provides complete onboarding guidance, checklists, administrator setup steps, learner quick-start instructions, manager review guidelines, and end-of-pilot closure procedures for pilot companies.

---

## 1. Pilot Overview & Checklist

- **Purpose**: Validate workplace sustainability micro-learning across company departments in a 4-week controlled pilot.
- **Scope**: Access to courses ELH-01 through ELH-29 in English and French.
- **Onboarding Checklist**:
  - [x] Nominate Company Administrator
  - [x] Nominate Department Managers
  - [x] Confirm Employee Roster & Language Preferences
  - [x] Review & Accept `company_pilot_notice`
  - [x] Assign Initial Core Sustainability Courses (ELH-01 to ELH-03)

---

## 2. Administrator & Manager Setup Guide

1. **First Login**: Access `/company` dashboard; accept Company Administrator Pilot Notice.
2. **Employee Roster**: Add employees individually via `/company/employees`.
3. **Course Assignment**: Select target courses and assign to departments.
4. **Progress Monitoring**: Track completion rates, overdue training, and average scores on `/company/reports`.
5. **Action Review**: Review workplace commitment evidence submissions under `/company/challenges-review`.

---

## 3. Learner Quick-Start Guide (EN / FR)

1. **Sign In**: Log into Elevio Skills using your corporate email.
2. **Language Preference**: Choose English or French from the top header navigation.
3. **Start Course**: Open assigned courses from your Dashboard. Complete interactive lessons and quizzes.
4. **Earn Certificate**: Achieve >= 80% on the end-of-course quiz to download your verified certificate PDF.
5. **Workplace Action**: Submit your workplace sustainability commitment and upload proof of implementation.

---

## 4. End-of-Pilot Process

1. **Final Reporting**: Export complete company training metrics via `GET /api/companies/export`.
2. **Feedback Collection**: Complete Administrator & Learner pilot feedback surveys.
3. **Transition**: Option to convert to a full commercial subscription or archive account data.
