# Company Administrator Onboarding Guide — EcoLearnHub

Welcome to EcoLearnHub! This guide explains how to complete your company setup, invite employees, assign your first sustainability course, and monitor learner activation.

---

## Step 1: Complete Your Company Profile
1. Log in as a Company Administrator and navigate to **Company Settings**.
2. Review and complete your organisation's trading name, industry sector, contact email, and reporting location.
3. Save your changes to update your corporate training profile.

---

## Step 2: Review Subscription & Employee Capacity
1. Go to **Subscription & Memberships**.
2. Confirm your current subscription plan (Essential, Professional, or Complete) and employee seat allowance.
3. Check your remaining capacity before adding or importing team members.

---

## Step 3: Invite Employees
You can invite team members individually or in bulk via CSV:

### Option A: Individual Invitation
1. Go to **Employees & Team**.
2. Click **Add Employee**.
3. Enter the employee's name, email, department, and role (`Learner`, `Manager`, or `Company Administrator`).
4. Click **Invite** to generate an invitation link.

### Option B: Bulk CSV Import
1. Go to **Employees & Team** and click **Import CSV**.
2. Download the standardized CSV template.
3. Fill in columns: `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`, `role`, `department`, `job_title`.
4. Upload your completed CSV file.
5. Review the row-validation summary and confirm import. Valid employees will receive invitation links automatically.

---

## Step 4: Manage Invitations
- **Resend Link**: Click **Resend Invitation** next to any pending invitation to generate a fresh link.
- **Revoke Access**: Click **Revoke Invitation** to cancel pending access for an employee.

---

## Step 5: Assign Your First Course
1. Go to **Course Assignments**.
2. Select **Assign First Course**.
3. Choose **ELH-01 — Sustainability Foundations** (recommended for all employees).
4. Select target employees or departments and click **Assign Course**.

---

## Step 6: Monitor Account Activation & Learning Progress
1. Open the **Company Dashboard** to see activation status and progress metrics.
2. Track who has accepted their invitation, started lessons, or completed their course.
