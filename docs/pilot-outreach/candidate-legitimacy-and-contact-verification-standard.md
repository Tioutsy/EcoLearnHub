# Candidate Legitimacy & Contact Verification Standard (Sprint 10G)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies acceptable proof of candidate organisation existence and contact authority level before outreach.

---

## Verification Requirements

1. **Official Corporate Identity**: Verifiable business registration or corporate website.
2. **Authorised Decision-Maker Contact**: Verified title (Sustainability Manager, HR Director, General Manager).
3. **Legitimate Business Channel**: Business email domain (`@coralbay.mu`, `@islandpro.mu`).
4. **Prohibition of Fictional / Seed Data**: Test seeds and internal demo accounts are strictly prohibited from live candidate classification.
