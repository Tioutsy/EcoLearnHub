# Pilot Participation Confirmation Standard (Sprint 10G)

## Executive Summary
This document specifies acceptable written participation evidence standards and rejects silent or unsupported acceptance claims.

---

## Confirmation Evidence Criteria

1. **Explicit Written Consent**: Signed agreement or formal business email confirmation.
2. **Authorised Representative**: Primary contact with verified authority.
3. **Exact Proposal Version**: Clear reference to accepted version (`v1` or `v2`).
4. **Prohibition of Silent Acceptance**: Silence, proposal opens, or meeting requests CANNOT be counted as participation confirmation.
